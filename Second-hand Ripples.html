Hover or tap a dot to read the passage.

Rings show how the writer places the information, not whether it was true. Hearsay means the writer gives no source, as in "we hear" or "they say". Link counts are those of the first coding run (coded.csv); a paper or notice counts as one link. Doubt is marked only where the writer's own words doubt the passage. Passages were found and coded by a local model with rule cues and have not been checked by hand.
